## What's new in SGVue 1.6.1

A fix to moving around the model. Nothing else changes.

**Pan and zoom follow what is under the cursor** — in the 3D (perspective) view, panning and the mouse wheel now take their speed from what is under the cursor, not from a hidden point the camera turns around. Two things this fixes:
- **Too slow.** After zooming in close to a wall, panning across a façade further away crept along at a fraction of the mouse's movement, and zooming towards it could stop. It now moves with the mouse.
- **Too fast.** After zooming out with the cursor on the ground, a pan slid the building faster than the mouse, and each scroll out dragged the view sideways into the site. The building now stays under the mouse.

Only a view-cube click used to put either right; now nothing needs to.

### What works differently after updating
- **With the cursor over empty site far from the building**, the wheel zooms towards the middle of the screen rather than towards the cursor, so the building stays in view.
- **Switching to orthographic after panning or zooming** sizes the orthographic view from what was last under the cursor, so it can come out a little larger or smaller than before.
- **Unchanged:** the plan and orthographic views' pan and zoom, touch and pinch, double-click to frame, the view cube and named views, zoom limits, clicking to select, saved viewpoints and share links.

### Known limits
- A project is saved with its models in the order they are open; a model that was missing when the project was saved is listed last.
- Ask Vee's IFC+SG answers name each element's IFC+SG identity, but never say whether a sub-type is valid or which parameters are required: the official IFC+SG mapping is not in SGVue.
- **New conversation** forgets the conversation, not what it did: hidden elements, colours and sections from earlier answers stay until you change them.
- A value in a derived unit is shown in SI units with no unit symbol in the Schedules window, and as written in the main window.
- SGVue is not code-signed on Windows, and on a Mac it is ad-hoc signed and not notarised by Apple yet (see below).

### System requirements
- **System** — Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit; or macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple silicon or an Intel processor.
- **Memory** — about 8 GB for IFC files up to about 100 MB, 16 GB for files up to about 300 MB, and 32 GB for larger files, up to the 600 MB limit. With several models open at once, add their sizes together.
- **Graphics** — any graphics card with WebGL2, which every PC of the last several years has; on a PC with an NVIDIA card, SGVue uses it automatically.
- **Files** — IFC, as `.ifc` or `.ifczip`, up to 600 MB a file. A file of up to about 300 MB opens in under half a minute; from 300 MB to 600 MB, expect 30–50 seconds. A file larger than 600 MB is not opened — split it into several models.
- **Disk** — about 119 MB to download, and about 390 MB once installed, on Windows. On a Mac with Apple silicon, about 129 MB to download and 336 MB once installed; on an Intel Mac, about 136 MB and 343 MB.
- **Internet** — not needed to view models. Two things use it: a check for a newer version when SGVue starts, which sends nothing about your files or models, and Ask Vee — only if you set it up with your own Anthropic API key.

The memory and opening times were measured with SGVue 1.3.0 on one PC — Windows 11 with 64 GB of memory and an 8 GB NVIDIA graphics card — with test models generated for the purpose. A slower PC takes longer to open a file, and a real model with less repeated geometry can need more memory.

### Install or update
On Windows, download `SGVue-1.6.1-setup.exe` below and run it. Updating keeps your settings, API key, recent files, saved views and saved templates. The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" — choose **More info → Run anyway**.

On a Mac, download `SGVue-1.6.1-arm64.dmg` (Apple silicon) or `SGVue-1.6.1-x64.dmg` (Intel) below, open it and drag SGVue onto Applications; to update, do the same and choose **Replace**. Updating keeps your settings, recent files, saved views and saved templates. SGVue is not notarised by Apple yet, so the first time you open it — and again after an update — macOS says it could not check the app: open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway** under Security, confirm, and enter your login password if asked.
