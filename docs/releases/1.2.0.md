## What's new in SGVue 1.2.0

**Vee** — the assistant has a name and a face. The panel and its button now read *Ask Vee*, and while Vee works you can watch it: it thinks, reads the models, filters to what you asked about and checks it — each step with its real count — then writes the answer and, when it has found elements, usually a row for each level that you can click to select them. The send button becomes a stop button while a reply is on its way, and your own messages now sit on the left, like Vee's.

**Vee can do much more of what you can do**
- It works the view for you: the canvas grid, snapping and original-materials switches, adding to or clearing the selection, undo and redo, showing and hiding whole models, the camera (fit, turn to any direction, zoom), saved viewpoints, one filter step changed in place, and the app's own settings such as theme and units.
- It lists your measurements and spot levels and zooms to one, and it can place a spot level or a laser measurement on an element you name — at the top, middle or base of the element's bounding box, not on a picked surface.
- In the Schedules window it sets column formats, calculated columns and the filter logic, applies templates, and saves, loads, renames and duplicates your saved setups, with that window's own undo and redo. It can also isolate, hide, select or colour in 3D exactly the rows the open schedule lists.
- A section Vee makes now cuts, as a click on the Section card does, and it can preview, flip, offset and clear either cut.
- **It asks first.** Opening or unloading a model, copying a link or GlobalIds, changing the base point, deleting a viewpoint, a measurement, a spot or a saved filter set — and, in the Schedules window, deleting a saved setup, printing and opening a schedule file — only happen when you click **Apply** in the chat, or answer the app's own question or dialog yourself.
- **revert** on a reply now puts back everything that reply changed in the view — the camera, a section, colours, the selection, a spot it placed — and not only what is visible. A viewpoint or filter set it saved, and a schedule it built, stay.
- As before, Vee cannot change your model, and it cannot reach your API key or Preferences.

**Two section cuts at once** — cut along a gridline and at a level at the same time. Each cut has its own offset, cut, flip side and Clear, and there is one Clear all. The gridline buttons are split by direction, for example A–E in one row and 1–10 in the next.

**A tidier window** — the toolbar is arranged in groups with dividers, and the Schedules button stands apart at the end. Undo, redo and the measurement and spot counts have a bar of their own above the status bar, and the pieces along the bottom edge of the 3D view — the status bar, the tool hints, the reset button and the Ask Vee button — no longer overlap one another. In the element tree the eye icons are on the left, as they are for storeys and models. Hover over a property that is cut off to read it in full, and over a model, a library row or a recent file to see its full path.

**Update notice** — SGVue's home page now tells you when a newer version is out, with a link to get it. SGVue asks GitHub once each time it starts; nothing about your files or models is sent, and when you are offline it simply stays quiet. Help › Check for updates now opens the SGVue web page, which says whether you are up to date.

**Fixes**
- The canvas grid sits at the model's own zero level. A model whose first part was high up used to hang under the grid.
- *Copy link*, *Copy GlobalId* and the chat table's *copy csv* now really copy.
- The chat no longer shows a sideways scrollbar.
- Asking Vee to activate a model that is already active no longer switches activate mode off.

### System requirements
- **System** — Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit; or macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple silicon or an Intel processor.
- **Memory** — 8 GB works for IFC files up to about 100 MB; 16 GB is recommended for 200 MB files, or for several models together.
- **Graphics** — any graphics card with WebGL2, which every PC of the last several years has; on a PC with an NVIDIA card, SGVue uses it automatically.
- **Files** — IFC, as `.ifc` or `.ifczip`. It is built for typical files of 50–200 MB; a single file larger than 600 MB is not opened.
- **Disk** — about 110 MB to download, and about 390 MB once installed, on Windows. On a Mac with Apple silicon, about 128 MB to download and 328 MB once installed; on an Intel Mac, about 135 MB and 335 MB.
- **Internet** — not needed to view models. Two things use it: a check for a newer version when SGVue starts, which sends nothing about your files or models, and Ask Vee — only if you set it up with your own Anthropic API key.

### Install or update
On Windows, download `SGVue-1.2.0-setup.exe` below and run it. Updating keeps your settings, API key, recent files and saved views. The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" — choose **More info → Run anyway**.

On a Mac, download `SGVue-1.2.0-arm64.dmg` (Apple silicon) or `SGVue-1.2.0-x64.dmg` (Intel) below, open it and drag SGVue onto Applications; to update, do the same and choose **Replace**. Updating keeps your settings, recent files and saved views. SGVue is not notarised by Apple yet, so the first time you open it — and again after an update — macOS says it could not check the app: open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway** under Security, confirm, and enter your login password if asked.
