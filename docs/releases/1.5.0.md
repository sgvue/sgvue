## What's new in SGVue 1.5.0

**A table-first Schedules window** — the Schedules window now gives the whole window to the table, after the redesign of ifcTable, the schedule tool it is built on. Click a column's heading for everything about that column in one menu: its heading text, total, decimals, unit, alignment, sorting, grouping, colour rules, a filter on it, its width and position, and hide or remove. Left and Right move between headings. Above the table, chips say what the schedule is doing — each filter as a sentence, with how many rows it removes, each sort and group level, **Rows** (itemise every instance, grand total) and **Style** — and each chip opens a small popover to change it. Add a column from **+ Column** at the end of the headings, or from the fields chip: a field picker you can search, showing how many elements carry each field.

**One Templates library** — the built-in templates and your own saved ones are now in one library, opened from the header's templates button, the **Templates library** button in the left panel, or the `T` key. Its list is in three sections — Mine, Built-in and IFC-SG — with a search and sortable columns, and its preview shows what the selected template gives on the models you have open: how many elements, which of its columns it finds and its first rows. Use, save, rename, duplicate, delete, export and import a template from the same place.

**A fresh set of icons** in the Schedules window, from ifcTable, drawn in SGVue's own style.

**Storeys follow the host** — a part, a nested component, or a door or window that only fills an opening in a wall, and is not placed on a storey itself, now stands on its host's storey, as IfcOpenShell reads it. It is counted under that storey in STOREYS, hidden with that storey's eye, matched by a Level rule, shown under it in the element tree and listed under it in the Schedules window and by Ask Vee. A model where every element is placed on a storey directly — most Revit exports — is unchanged.

**Schedules read the file more exactly**
- A value in one of the file's own derived units — a thermal transmittance or a flow rate, say — is shown in SI units in the Schedules window, with any mass in it counted in kilograms. The main window still shows it as the file writes it.
- Building, Site and Room / Space come from where the element itself is placed: a wall placed in the building names the building, and furniture placed in a room names the room.
- Placeholder values — `-`, `—`, `n/a`, `none` or a blank — count as no value where the Schedules window counts which elements carry a field.

**Fixes**
- A mass per length and a rotational mass in the Schedules window are no longer converted as if they were a length or a mass.
- A group footer or grand total whose first total is a zero hidden by "Blank instead of zero" now shows its label on screen, as the Excel and CSV exports already did.
- Saving a template under a numbered name — `Door Schedule (2)` — or renaming the saved template on screen no longer brings the old name back when you press Undo.
- A schedule Ask Vee changes is always its own Undo step, even when it arrives while you are typing.
- If the Schedules window does not answer Ask Vee in time, Vee now says it is not known whether the action happened, and to check before asking again, instead of saying it most likely did not.

**Security**
- An Excel export writes every cell as plain text or a number, so nothing in a doctored template can become a live formula or link in the workbook.
- A schedule's calculated columns are rebuilt from known fields however the schedule arrives — a `.schedule.json`, a saved template, a project file or Ask Vee — and anything else in them is dropped.

### What works differently after updating
- **The Schedules window's right-hand panel is gone.** What was in it is now here:
  - **Fields** — add a column from **+ Column** at the end of the headings or the fields chip above the table, and reorder the columns by dragging them in the picker's **In this table** list; hide, remove or move one from its heading's menu.
  - **Filter** — the filter chips above the table and **+ Filter**, or **Filter by this…** in a column's menu.
  - **Sort** — the sort chips and **+ Sort**, or the sort buttons and **Group by this** in a column's menu.
  - **Format** — the column's own menu: click its heading.
  - **Style** — the **Style** chip. Itemise and the grand total are in the **Rows** chip.
  - The calculated-value editor opens as a dialog of its own, from the field picker's **+ Calculated value** or a calculated column's **Edit formula**.
- **The template gallery and My templates are one Templates library.** Your saved templates are all there, under **Mine**.
- **At 100 saved templates, saving or duplicating one is refused** — "Saved templates are full (100). Delete one to make room." SGVue 1.4.0 dropped the oldest to make room; nothing is dropped now.
- **Importing a `.schedule.json` adds it to Mine** and leaves the schedule on screen as it was. Choose it in the library and **Use** it to open it.
- **A between filter with one box empty is open-ended.** With only "to" filled it keeps values at most that; with only "from", values at least that; with both empty it keeps every row. SGVue 1.4.0 read an empty box as 0, so a schedule saved with one end empty now keeps different rows — check its filter chip.
- **Nested parts, and doors and windows that only fill an opening, are on their host's storey.** In a model that places them that way, STOREYS counts, a storey's eye, Level rules and schedules grouped by level now include them, where 1.4.0 left them on no storey.
- **The Schedules window is at least 780 px wide** (it was 560 px), so the table, its chips and its menus have room.

### Known limits
- A value in a derived unit is shown in SI units with no unit symbol in the Schedules window, and as written in the main window.
- SGVue is not code-signed on Windows, and on a Mac it is ad-hoc signed and not notarised by Apple yet (see below).

### System requirements
- **System** — Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit; or macOS 13 Ventura or later, on Apple silicon or an Intel processor.
- **Memory** — about 8 GB for IFC files up to about 100 MB, 16 GB for files up to about 300 MB, and 32 GB for larger files, up to the 600 MB limit. With several models open at once, add their sizes together.
- **Graphics** — any graphics card with WebGL2, which every PC of the last several years has; on a PC with an NVIDIA card, SGVue uses it automatically.
- **Files** — IFC, as `.ifc` or `.ifczip`, up to 600 MB a file. A file of up to about 300 MB opens in under half a minute; from 300 MB to 600 MB, expect 30–50 seconds. A file larger than 600 MB is not opened — split it into several models.
- **Disk** — the download and installed sizes are given when the installers are published.
- **Internet** — not needed to view models. Two things use it: a check for a newer version when SGVue starts, which sends nothing about your files or models, and Ask Vee — only if you set it up with your own Anthropic API key.

The memory and opening times were measured with SGVue 1.3.0 on one PC — Windows 11 with 64 GB of memory and an 8 GB NVIDIA graphics card — with test models generated for the purpose. A slower PC takes longer to open a file, and a real model with less repeated geometry can need more memory.

### Install or update
On Windows, download `SGVue-1.5.0-setup.exe` below and run it. Updating keeps your settings, API key, recent files, saved views and saved templates. The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" — choose **More info → Run anyway**.

On a Mac, download `SGVue-1.5.0-arm64.dmg` (Apple silicon) or `SGVue-1.5.0-x64.dmg` (Intel) below, open it and drag SGVue onto Applications; to update, do the same and choose **Replace**. Updating keeps your settings, recent files, saved views and saved templates. SGVue is not notarised by Apple yet, so the first time you open it — and again after an update — macOS says it could not check the app: open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway** under Security, confirm, and enter your login password if asked.
